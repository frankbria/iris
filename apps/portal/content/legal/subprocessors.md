These are the third parties that process personal data on IRIS's behalf. How we use data is described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy); our obligations to your organization are in the [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa).

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Data | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linode (Akamai) | Hosting the service: servers, storage and network | All service data, including accounts, results and backups | United States |
| Resend | Sending verification, password-reset and invitation emails | Recipient email address, sign-in links, inviter and organization names | United States |
| OpenAI (only when your organization chooses IRIS credits) | AI instruction translation and visual comparison | Instructions, page URLs, screenshots | United States |
| Anthropic (only when your organization chooses IRIS credits) | AI instruction translation and visual comparison | Instructions, page URLs, screenshots | United States |
| Stripe | Payments and invoicing | Billing contact, payment details, usage totals | United States |

## Your own AI provider key (not a subprocessor)

Unless your organization chooses IRIS credits, IRIS contacts OpenAI or Anthropic only with your organization's own key ("bring your own key"). Those requests run on your organization's own account with the vendor, which processes the data under your organization's contract with it, not as our subprocessor. Nothing is sent to a vendor your organization has not added a key for. OpenAI and Anthropic appear in the table above only for IRIS credits, which an organization's owners or admins choose on the AI provider keys page.

## Changes

We will publish changes to this list here, with a new version. Before we add or replace a subprocessor, we update this page and email the owners of every organization at least 30 days in advance. An organization may object within that period, as set out in the [Data Processing Agreement](/dpa).
