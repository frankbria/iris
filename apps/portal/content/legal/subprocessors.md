## About the AI vendors

Today IRIS contacts OpenAI or Anthropic only with your organization's own key ("bring your own key"). Those requests run on your organization's account with the vendor, under your agreement with it, and nothing is sent to a vendor your organization has not added a key for. When IRIS-provided AI credits launch, requests using them will run on our account, and the vendor will then act as our subprocessor for them.
