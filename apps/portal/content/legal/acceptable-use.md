This policy is a working draft and is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms). IRIS drives real browsers against real websites, so what you point it at matters. Text in [square brackets] is a placeholder.

## 1. Only test what you may test

- Use IRIS only on sites, applications and systems you own, or that the owner has authorised you to test.
- Do not point IRIS at someone else's site to see how it looks, copy it, or probe it without that permission.

## 2. What you must not do

- **Attack or overload a target.** No denial of service, load or stress testing without the owner's written consent, and no scanning for or exploiting vulnerabilities in systems you are not authorised to test.
- **Credential attacks.** No credential stuffing, password guessing, account takeover or testing of stolen credentials.
- **Scraping against a site's rules.** No collecting content or data in breach of a site's terms, robots rules or the law, and no bulk harvesting of personal data.
- **Malware and abuse.** No distributing malware, phishing, spam, or fraud, and no use of IRIS to hide where an attack comes from.
- **Illegal or harmful content.** No content or activity that is illegal where you or the target are, including content that exploits or endangers children, and no infringement of others' rights.
- **Evading our limits.** No trying to bypass IRIS's network protections (for example its limits on reaching private, internal or metadata addresses), its egress controls, rate limits, quotas or budgets, and no using IRIS to reach systems we have deliberately blocked.
- **Abusing the platform.** No interfering with the service or other customers, probing IRIS for weaknesses without our written permission, or sharing access in a way that avoids limits.
- **Reselling.** No selling or offering IRIS access to others as a service without our written permission.

## 3. Keys and credentials

- Any AI provider key you give IRIS must be one you are entitled to use. Do not submit keys that belong to someone else.
- Keep your API keys secret. If one leaks, revoke it in the portal.
- Do not put real third-party credentials into tests. Use test accounts.

## 4. Enforcement

We may refuse or stop a run, limit or suspend an account, or remove content that breaks this policy, and may report illegal activity to the authorities. We try to give notice where we reasonably can, but may act at once to stop harm. To report abuse or a suspected vulnerability in IRIS: [security and abuse contact].

## 5. Changes

This policy is versioned like the terms. A new version is published here and you are asked to accept it at your next sign-in.
